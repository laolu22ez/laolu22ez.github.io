Who I Am

Creativity is how I solve problems.

I am an Information Technology student at York University with a love for innovation and creativity. Since a young age from crayon drawings, to Microsoft Paint, learning about philosophy or designing interactive maps for a real university collective — creativity has always been my way of life.

What sets me apart is my love for the game, in anything I set my mind to I give 111%. My background spans web development, software programming, video production, and hands-on community tech support — a rare combination that lets me bridge the gap between technical execution and human impact.

I contributed to the S4 Collective for two years, designing an interactive Sanctuary Student campus map used to help marginalized students navigate educational systems across Ontario. I also earned a certificate from the University of Paris through the GRABS program, where I edited and recorded a podcast on growing up across borders — proof that I can communicate across cultural and technical divides alike.

I speak English and French fluently as well as Yoruba. I bring the same meticulous attention to detail whether I'm debugging JavaScript, advising a customer, or training a new colleague. I adapt fast, take ownership, and I genuinely care about the work I produce.
